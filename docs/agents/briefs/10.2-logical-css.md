## What to build
Scope (grep on 2026-10-08, approximate): about 140 physical utilities in 32 `.tsx` files; about 115 of them in 20 files
under `src/components/ui` (`sidebar.tsx` 17 lines, `menubar.tsx`, `dropdown-menu.tsx`, `context-menu.tsx` 9 each,
`sheet.tsx` 7, `carousel.tsx` and `calendar.tsx` 6 each). Outside it: `radar.tsx` 4, and 1–2 each in `index.tsx`, `auth.tsx`,
`today.tsx`, `_authenticated/route.tsx`, `resume.tsx`, `connections.tsx`, `applications*.tsx`, `ReviewQueue.tsx`,
`GmailSetupHelp.tsx`, `AgentPanel.tsx`. Re-run the grep first; mind false hits such as `rounded-lg`.
1. Replace, including variants (`sm:`, `data-[…]:`, `group-…:`) and negatives: `ml-`/`mr-` → `ms-`/`me-`, `pl-`/`pr-` →
   `ps-`/`pe-`, `left-`/`right-` → `start-`/`end-`, `text-left`/`text-right` → `text-start`/`text-end`, `border-l`/`border-r` →
   `border-s`/`border-e`, `rounded-l`/`rounded-r` (and `rounded-tl` etc.) → `rounded-s`/`rounded-e` (`rounded-ss` etc.).
   The project uses Tailwind 4.2: check whether `start-*`/`end-*` are deprecated there in favour of `inset-s-*`/`inset-e-*`,
   and use the current form consistently.
2. Not covered by a plain rename, handle by hand: `slide-in-from-left/right` and `slide-out-to-*` animations (sheet, menus;
   about 15 lines) via `rtl:` variants; `translate-x` and `-translate-x` (sidebar rail, switch thumb); `space-x-*` (use `gap`);
   the `side` prop logic in `sidebar.tsx` and `sheet.tsx` so "left" means the start edge; `carousel.tsx` (Embla `direction`
   option from `dir`); `calendar.tsx` (react-day-picker `dir` prop).
3. Directional icons (about 36 lines use `ChevronLeft/Right` or `ArrowLeft/Right`): add `rtl:rotate-180` only where the icon
   means "back/forward/next/submenu" (breadcrumb, pagination, carousel, submenu chevrons), never to `ChevronDown` or to
   icons that point at a fixed thing.
4. Leave intentionally physical cases (charts, the `.ats-paper` CV preview whose direction 10.6 owns) with a
   `// physical: <reason>` comment.
5. Commit in two steps for review (`src/components/ui`, then the app files). If the diff is too big for one review, split into
   10.2a (`src/components/ui`) and 10.2b (the rest), merged back to back, never in parallel with other UI work.
No behaviour, text or logic changes in this task.
