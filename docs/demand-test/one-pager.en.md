# Your job search, handled honestly

**Nexus** is a job-search assistant that does the tiring parts of looking for work and leaves every decision to you.

## What it does
- **Finds roles and prepares your application.** It collects roles that fit you, tailors your CV for each, and writes a short cover letter, using only things that are true in your own records. It never invents experience.
- **Asks you before anything is sent.** You see each prepared application, then approve, edit or skip it, from your phone if you like. Nothing is sent without your yes.
- **Watches your inbox for replies.** When a company answers (an interview invitation, a rejection, a request for more information), it updates that application for you, so you always know where each one stands.
- **Reminds you at the right time.** If a week has passed with no reply, it drafts a polite follow-up for you to send.
- **Works in Arabic and English.** The screens, the CV and the letters can be in either language.

## What it will never do
- Pretend to be you, or claim skills or results you don't have.
- Send an application, or answer a question about your visa, salary or legal status, without your approval.
- Log in to LinkedIn or other job sites on your behalf, or use tricks that put your accounts at risk.
- Read your personal mail: only messages that look like job mail are read, and you can delete everything at any time.

## What we would like to learn from you
We are not selling anything today. We want to understand how you search for work, what is hard, and whether something like this would help. Your answers are anonymous, and there are no right answers.
