# Interview guide (20 minutes)

Run each interview alone with one person, in Arabic or English as they prefer. Keep to the clock: the pricing
questions at the end matter most and are the first to be squeezed. **Ask, then stay quiet.** Write down their
words, not your summary. Arabic wording is given for the questions that matter; adapt it to the dialect you are using.

## 0. Opening (2 min)
Say: *"I'm testing an idea, not selling anything. There are no right answers, and if something sounds useless to you,
that's the most helpful thing you can tell me. I'll take notes by hand and I won't write your name anywhere."*
Ask for their OK to take notes. Record audio only if they clearly agree, and keep it outside this repository.

> «أنا أختبر فكرة ولا أبيع شيئًا. لا توجد إجابات صحيحة، وإذا بدا لك شيء غير مفيد فهذا أنفع ما يمكن أن تقوله لي. سأكتب ملاحظات بخط اليد ولن أكتب اسمك في أي مكان.»

Note their **role or field**, **country**, and **how long they have been searching** (no name).

## 1. How they search today (5 min)
Ask them to walk you through the **last time** they applied for a job, step by step.
- Where did you find it? What did you open next? What did you send, and how?
- How many applications did you send last month? How many replies came back? How do you know?
- Where do you keep track of what you applied to?

> «احكِ لي عن آخر مرة قدّمت فيها على وظيفة، خطوة بخطوة. من أين وجدتها؟ ماذا أرسلت؟ وكيف تتابع ما قدّمت عليه؟»

Listen for: the sites and apps they use, copy-paste work, lost replies, forgotten follow-ups.
Don't suggest answers.

## 2. What wastes their time (4 min)
- What is the most tiring or annoying part of looking for work?
- What have you tried to make it easier (tools, templates, a friend, an agency, AI chat)? What happened?
- Have you ever missed a reply, or applied twice, or forgotten to follow up? What did it cost you?

> «ما أكثر جزء مرهق في البحث عن عمل؟ وماذا جرّبت لتسهيله؟ وهل فاتك رد من شركة أو نسيت أن تتابع؟»

## 3. Reaction to the idea (5 min)
Show the one-pager, then the prototype (`prototype/index.html`), screen by screen. Say once that it is **made up and
not built**. After each screen ask only: *"What do you think this does? What would you change?"*
1. **Today's queue.** Would you open this every day? What would you want to see first?
2. **Approving an application.** Would you trust it to prepare this? What would you need to check before approving?
   (Note whether "only things true in your records" matters to them.)
3. **Tracking replies.** Would you let it read your job-related mail? What would worry you? What would make you
   comfortable? (Note which mailbox they use.)

> «هل تفتح هذه الشاشة كل يوم؟ هل تثق بأن يجهّز لك الطلب؟ وما الذي ستتحقق منه قبل أن توافق؟ وهل تسمح له بقراءة بريدك الخاص بالوظائف؟ وما الذي يقلقك؟»

## 4. Approving in a messaging app (2 min)
- If you could approve or skip each prepared application from WhatsApp or Telegram, would you? Which one, and why?
- How would you feel about sending your CV through a bot there?

> «لو استطعت أن توافق على كل طلب أو تتخطاه من واتساب أو تيليجرام، هل تفضّل ذلك؟ ولماذا؟»

## 5. Use and price (2 min)
Ask these **in this order**, and write the numbers they say before you show any price of yours.
1. *"During a job search, would you use something like this every week? What would stop you?"*
   > «خلال فترة بحثك عن عمل، هل ستستخدم شيئًا كهذا كل أسبوع؟ وما الذي قد يمنعك؟»
2. *"At what monthly price would this feel so cheap that you'd doubt it? A bargain? Expensive but still possible? Too
   expensive?"* (local currency)
   > «عند أي سعر شهري يبدو لك رخيصًا لدرجة الشك؟ صفقة جيدة؟ غاليًا لكن ممكن؟ غاليًا جدًا؟»
3. Then show the price you are testing: *"If it cost X a month, would you pay for it? Would you pay this week?"*
   > «لو كان سعره (X) شهريًا، هل تدفع؟ وهل تدفع هذا الأسبوع؟»
4. *"Who else would pay for it: you, an employer, a university, a family member?"*

## 6. Close (<1 min)
- *"What did I not ask that I should have?"*
- *"Who else searching for work could I talk to?"* (Only note a first name or "a friend"; no contact details here.)
- Thank them. Don't promise a product or a date.

## After the interview (5 min, straight away)
Fill in [`notes-template.md`](notes-template.md) while it is fresh. Keep the filled sheets **outside this repository**.
