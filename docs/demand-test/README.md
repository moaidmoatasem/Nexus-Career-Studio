# Demand test kit (PLAN.md task B.2)

Everything needed to test whether job seekers in Egypt and the Gulf want a hosted, Arabic-first job-search agent.
It contains **no personal data** and no real employer data: the prototype uses made-up people and companies.

| File | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [`one-pager.ar.md`](one-pager.ar.md), [`one-pager.en.md`](one-pager.en.md) | A one-page description of the product in Arabic and English | Send or show before or during an interview |
| [`interview-guide.md`](interview-guide.md) | A 20-minute interview script with Arabic wording for the key questions | Run each interview |
| [`notes-template.md`](notes-template.md) | A one-page note sheet that scores each interview against your pass thresholds | Fill in right after each interview |
| [`prototype/index.html`](prototype/index.html) | A clickable three-screen prototype (today's queue, approving an application, tracking replies), English and Arabic | Open in any browser and walk the interviewee through it |

## Before the first interview
1. **[owner] Set the pass thresholds in PLAN.md task B.1.** The note sheet asks the two questions the suggested
   thresholds need (would use weekly, would pay a stated price), so the kit works with whatever numbers you choose.
2. Decide the monthly price you will test, in local currency, for each market (Egypt, Gulf). The guide asks
   interviewees to name prices first and only then shows yours, so you can compare.
3. Open the prototype once on the device you will use (a phone works) and check both languages.

## Have the Arabic reviewed
The Arabic was written by an AI assistant. Before the first interview, ask a native speaker from your target market to
read the one-pager, the interview questions and the prototype, and to fix the dialect, tone and any awkward phrasing.

## Rules that keep the test honest
- Interview people who **don't use developer tools**, across several kinds of role, not only tech.
- Take notes by hand. Record only with the person's clear consent, and keep recordings and notes **outside this
  repository**; the note sheet uses a participant number, never a name.
- The prototype shows a **possible** product. Say so. Nothing in it is built, and nothing is submitted for anyone.
- Don't sell. Ask what they do today and what they would do, and write down what they say, including "no".
